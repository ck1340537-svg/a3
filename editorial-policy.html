Editorial Policy

How we develop, test and correct content on Caper Juniper.

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Readers trust our recipes and guides to work and to be safe. These standards guide everything we publish.

Testing

Recipes and brine ratios are tested in a home kitchen, usually more than once, before publication. We note the equipment used and any variations that worked well.

Food safety first

Pickling and preserving advice is checked against established guidance from public food-safety authorities. We clearly label refrigerator methods and never present untested methods as suitable for shelf storage.

Writing standards

  • Ingredients are listed clearly with measurements.
  • Methods explain what to look for at each stage.
  • We avoid health claims and focus on flavour and technique.
  • We describe ingredients and their origins accurately.

Independence

We do not accept payment, free products or affiliate commissions in exchange for coverage. Advertisers, if present, have no influence over our content.

Corrections

If you spot an error, please tell us through our contact page. We review every report, correct confirmed mistakes promptly and update the page date for significant changes.
