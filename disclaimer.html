Food safety

Quick pickles described on this website are refrigerator pickles. They must be kept chilled and are best eaten within a few weeks. They are not suitable for storage at room temperature. For shelf-stable canned foods, use only tested recipes and methods from recognised food-safety authorities. Discard any preserved food that smells off, looks slimy or cloudy in an unusual way, or shows mould.

Brine calculator

The calculator provides general proportions for flavour. Results depend on your ingredients, jar size and how tightly vegetables are packed. Always use vinegar of at least 5% acidity and keep pickles refrigerated.
