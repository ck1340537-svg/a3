Privacy Policy

How Caper Juniper collects, uses and protects your information.

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Caper Juniper (“we”, “us”, “our”) runs www.caperjuniper.com, a free food website about pickling, spices and pantry cooking. This policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it and the choices you have.

Information you provide

When you use our contact form, we receive your name, email address, topic and message. When you subscribe to The Pantry Post, we receive your email address. We use this information only to reply to you or to send the newsletter. We do not sell products and never ask for payment details.

Information collected automatically

We automatically receive technical information such as IP address, browser and device type, operating system, referring website, pages viewed and visit times. Our brine calculator and pairing finder work entirely in your browser and do not send your choices to us. Cookies are explained in our Cookie Policy.

How we use information

  • To respond to questions, feedback and corrections.
  • To send the newsletter to subscribers.
  • To understand which guides are most useful and improve them.
  • To keep the website secure and free from spam.
  • To meet legal obligations.

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to measure how visitors use our website. It uses cookies to collect information such as pages visited and time spent, processed by Google under its own policies; see How Google uses information from sites that use its services. You can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Advertising

This website may show ads served by Google and other third-party vendors. These vendors use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings or www.aboutads.info/choices.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us operate the website, such as hosting and email delivery, or where required by law.

Retention

We keep messages only as long as needed to respond. Newsletter addresses are kept until you unsubscribe. Analytics data is retained according to our Google Analytics settings.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under the GDPR and CCPA, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information. Contact us to make a request; each newsletter also includes an unsubscribe link.

Children

Our website is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. Please contact us if you believe a child has provided personal data.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards, including HTTPS encryption. No online system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date above shows the latest version.
