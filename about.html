Our story

About Caper Juniper

A small, independent food website for anyone who loves bright, punchy flavours and wants to bring a little more of them into everyday cooking.

How it began

A jar of capers and a cabbage

Caper Juniper started with two ingredients most people leave at the back of the cupboard. A spoonful of capers rescued a dull pasta; a few crushed juniper berries turned braised cabbage into something people asked for again. We wanted to know why such small things made such a big difference.

That curiosity grew into this website: a practical guide to pickling, spices and pantry staples, written for home cooks who want more flavour without more fuss.

Practical first

Every guide is built around something you can make tonight with ordinary equipment.

Safety matters

We explain food-safety basics clearly, especially for pickling and preserving.

Independent

We do not sell products or accept paid placements. Our recommendations are our own.

How we work

Tested in a home kitchen

Recipes and brine ratios are tested in an ordinary kitchen and checked against established food-safety guidance. We say clearly which methods are for the fridge and which require tested canning procedures.
